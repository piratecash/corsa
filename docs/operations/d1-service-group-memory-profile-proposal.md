Before the daemon starts, acquire verified read-only cgroup FDs and retain its identity, actual memory.max/cpu.max/pids.max/no-swap configuration, process membership and initial memory.peak/events. In a fresh cgroup initial `oom`, `oom_kill` and `oom_group_kill` must be zero; never hide early OOM by baselining it away. Do not reset peak counters. Bind keeper, daemon and sampler by PID/birth/executable and actual cgroup membership; hold CURRENT/FOR_CHILDREN namespace FDs and validate equality inside the service group before useful work and at bookends. No zero-offset exception or clock translation.

Proposed cadence500ms applies from sampler-ready before daemon launch through daemon reap, including both edge gaps; actual gaps over2seconds or missing records make coverage incomplete. Later sampler join and keeper seal are not scheduled500ms samples: continuous kernel peak/events have their separate creation-to-seal window. Keep ordered NDJSON, sample/read brackets, CPU counters, cgroup memory.current/peak/events and sampled per-process RSS/HWM/PSS as distinct quantities. Hash the qualified daemon once before useful work and once afterwards, with held executable identity checks during sampling; do not reuse `LinuxProcessSampler.Sample`'s repeated full-ELF hashes as a high-frequency collector.

Stop/reap daemon and sampler with normal exit0, then while keeper remains alive read final kernel peak/current/events and actual membership. This is not an atomic snapshot: an owner-local read bracket bounds the peak observation; events reads before and after peak must stay0, and current has its separate bracket. The selected window ends at this bounded peak observation, not an invented common exact instant. Only keeper may remain; unknown descendants invalidate the seal. The selected peak covers fresh cgroup creation through this final read, including the entire daemon and sampler lifetimes. **Keeper-only exit-tail peak/OOM after that read remain UNKNOWN.** Hard limits remain enforced until container exit, but do not call the observed value a full-group-lifetime peak. Normal exit0/reap for daemon/driver/sampler/keeper, final RAW/EOF, owned-volume/container removal and fresh absence queries remain required. Forced removal can establish scoped cleanup after refusal, but never substitutes for successful useful work.

Sampled RSS/HWM/PSS are optional attribution metrics: unavailable values remain explicitly UNKNOWN. Mandatory peak/events, identity, workload and coverage evidence cannot be omitted.

A scoped success requires the exact useful workload completed before deadlines, complete coverage, unchanged identity/cgroup, no OOM events through the seal, a valid kernel peak≤512000000 and normal owned exits/cleanup. A low peak obtained by starving work is not success. Missing/unreadable/reset counters, changed identity or clock domain, lost final output or unknown closure yield INCOMPLETE/UNKNOWN; observed functional failure/OOM is FAIL. Publish raw failure facts without erasing them behind a single verdict.

Primary metric is kernel **charged cgroup peak in the declared window**, including all three service roles. It is not a sum of RSS, an isolated corsad/sampler charge, a physical shared-page upper bound or co-tenant safety proof.
